What the model is using

    Completed and unverified shipment weeks
    Model 1: the calculation in plain language

    Start with the customer's forecast. Learn how its past errors relate to the forecast size, weeks until delivery, and revisions. Ridge regression learns the coefficients and shrinks them toward zero when the evidence is weak.

    Raw model = max(0, customer forecast + scale × learned correction) Final prediction = customer forecast + blend × (raw model − customer forecast) scale = mean of available actuals in the prior 13 weeks (at least 1) If no actuals are available: median positive forecast in that snapshot. blend = 0, 0.25, 0.5 or 1, selected using earlier walk-forward predictions. blend = 0 keeps the customer's original forecast.

    The small model needs at least 12 distinct completed target weeks and eight origins. The revision model needs 26 target weeks and eight origins. Corrections also need earlier out-of-sample evidence and at least 2% lower validation error, including six supported cases at the exact delivery horizon. These are conservative starting rules, not a guarantee.
